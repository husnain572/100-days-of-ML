## Difference Between AI, ML, and DL

### 1. Artificial Intelligence (AI)
- Broad field of creating machines that can mimic human intelligence.
- Includes reasoning, problem-solving, decision-making.
- Can be rule-based (no learning required).

**Example:**
- Chess-playing program using predefined rules.

### 2. Machine Learning (ML)
- Subset of AI.
- Machines learn patterns from data instead of being explicitly programmed.
- Improves performance with experience (data).

**Example:**
- Email spam classifier trained on labeled emails.

### 3. Deep Learning (DL)
- Subset of ML.
- Uses neural networks with many layers (deep neural networks).
- Works well with large and complex data (images, audio, text).

**Example:**
- Image recognition system (cat vs dog classifier).


## Key Differences

| Feature            | AI                          | ML                          | DL                          |
|------------------|-----------------------------|-----------------------------|-----------------------------|
| Scope            | Broadest                    | Subset of AI                | Subset of ML                |
| Learning         | Not always required         | Learns from data            | Learns from large data      |
| Techniques       | Rules, logic, search        | Algorithms (Regression, KNN)| Neural Networks             |
| Data Requirement | Low to high                 | Moderate                    | Very high                   |
| Complexity       | Low to high                 | Moderate                    | High                        |
